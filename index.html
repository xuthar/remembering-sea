01 / A LETTER TO THE OCEAN

AN OCEAN OF MEMORIES

There are places
we leave.
And places that
never leave us.

✧

For every shore we have walked in silence. For the wind that knew our names. For the days that drifted away, and the moments that still return with the tide.

04 / WORDS ADRIFT
A QUIET KIND OF INFINITY

THE BOOK OF TIDES

Words the sea
couldn't take.

I returned when the beaches were empty,
when the light had forgotten the sand.
The water said nothing about leaving.
It simply reached for my hand.

— FROM “THE SHORE THAT STAYED”
